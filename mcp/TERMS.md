## 2. What you may do with NEXA Care directory results

- Show them to an individual user inside an AI assistant or app, in answer to that user's question.
- Quote them in a reply, keeping the attribution “Source: NEXA Longevity (nxlongevity.com)” and the `as_of` date.

## 3. What you may not do

- Copy, store or rebuild the directory in bulk, or crawl the server to do so.
- Republish NEXA Care results under an open licence, sell them, or include them in a dataset or a model's training data.
- Remove the source, date or disclaimer, or present results as an endorsement, ranking guarantee or confirmed booking.
- Send patient information to this server. It is designed not to receive any.

## 4. No advice, no guarantee

Results are information only, not medical, legal or immigration advice. A listing does not mean a doctor or
hospital is available to a given patient, accepts international patients, or has agreed to work with NEXA.
Always verify licences through official channels (see `verification_guide`).

The server is free, provided as-is, and may be rate-limited, changed or withdrawn. Records may be removed
at any time, for example when a permission expires. NEXA may update these terms; the version in this file applies.
